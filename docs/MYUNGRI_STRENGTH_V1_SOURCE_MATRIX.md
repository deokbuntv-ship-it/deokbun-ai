> listed here with its sourcing, school-dependence, conflicts, and canonical status. `CANONICAL_STATUS` values:

> documented, or an engine-fact-coverage gap must be closed first), `REJECT` (Level D, or structurally
> incompatible with the frozen kernel/red-flags), `DEFER` (SOURCE_GAP too wide to adopt or reject yet).
>
> No fabricated source locations. Where a precise passage/page cannot be confidently cited, `SOURCE_EXCERPT_OR_
> LOCATION` says so explicitly rather than inventing one — this column names the text and general doctrinal
> area, not a page-verified quotation, unless stated otherwise.

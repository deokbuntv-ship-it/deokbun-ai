# COUNTEREXAMPLE REGISTER

> Seed rules R1–R12 are **hypotheses to attack**, not candidate doctrine. None is adopted. Several are
> already known to be false; they are kept precisely so the corpus accumulates the evidence that refutes
> them, in a form a later gate can cite.

> **⚠️ S1.5 SCOPE WARNING — added after the second lineage was examined.**
> Every status below marked REFUTED at S1 was established on **one lineage**
> (滴天髓闡微 / 任鐵樵). The first cross-lineage check immediately overturned one
> of them: **R11 is refuted in 滴天髓 and asserted as doctrine in 淵海子平**
> (`CF-011`). Until each rule has been checked against a second source family,
> read every verdict here as *"refuted **in this lineage**"*, not *"refuted"*.
> A lineage-specific doctrine is indistinguishable from a universal one when you
> only have one lineage — that is precisely what S1.5 exists to correct, and it
> is already producing corrections.

## Failure classification (§31 of the governing plan)

A counterexample does **not** automatically make a rule universally false. Every hit is classified:

`WRONG_RULE` · `MISSING_PRECONDITION` · `SCHOOL_SPECIFIC` · `MISREAD_SOURCE` · `CASE_AMBIGUOUS` ·
`TRANSCRIPTION_ERROR` · `RELATION_EFFECT_MISSING` · `SPECIAL_PATTERN_TRANSITION`

## The pattern these already show

Most of the ten recorded counterexamples come from **matched or paired constructions** where 任鐵樵 sets up a
chart that a mechanical reading gets wrong, then says so explicitly (似乎…然 · 俗論…不知 · 故特書兩造為後證 ·
非身弱論也 · 非身衰論也).

Two further structures appear once the special-pattern chapters are included:

- **Explicit denial of the naive category.** Twice the commentator states not that the naive reading is
  mis-weighted but that it is the *wrong kind of claim*: 非身弱論也 (`CE-006`), 非身衰論也 (`CE-008`). 從 is
  not the far end of a weakness scale. V1's seven-band ladder had no way to represent that.
- **Near-identical pairs with opposite outcomes.** `DTS-HUAXIANG-03` / `-04` differ by one character, and
  the chart with the *truer* transformation fares worse. `DTS-SHUNJU-03` / `-04`: 大象觀之勝於前造, outcome
  worse. Any axis that is monotone in its inputs cannot reproduce these.

This is a methodological finding worth stating early: **the source is itself adversarial against
formulaic strength rules.** V1 tried to extract a formula from a text whose author repeatedly warns against
formulas. Any V2 axis must be able to reproduce these inversions, or it has learned the wrong thing.

**No rule is adopted from this file.** Cases accumulate; conclusions wait for S2.

mechanism of the disagreement is fully explicit on both sides.

**INDEPENDENCE_ASSESSMENT**: B's preface dates itself 乙亥 (summer 1935). A's

no textual awareness of B (A asserts 曲直仁壽格 outright, exactly what B had

so the chart *datum* likely circulated publicly even though the *reasoning* did
not; and both readings are plausibly retrodictive (徐 knows the subject is a
head of state and returns nobility; 韋's client had a mediocre life and 韋
returns a broken 格).

**BRIDGE_CLASSIFICATION**: `SPECIAL_PATTERN_CONFLICT` — a genuine entry-test

**PRODUCT_RELEVANCE**: **CRITICAL.** A special-pattern engine that ignores

abstract dispute.

presented as "the" classical rule.

**PRODUCT_RELEVANCE**: **HIGH**, specifically for TIMING. This is direct
evidence that two competent named authorities can agree on every structural fact

**PROPOSITION_B**: 胞胎格 — a fragile, barely-alive structure. **印綬 is

**SAME_PROPOSITION?** On its face, **SAME_PROPOSITION_DIFFERENT_ANSWER**, exact

**THE DEFECT.** This chart drew four separate verifications in the workflow,
and they disagree with each other. One verifier ran the decisive negative
control — does 萬民英's own corpus elsewhere assign this chart to 從煞, which
would collapse "two authorities disagree" into "one author is internally
inconsistent"? Searching for a *contiguous* eight-character run across 三命通會

result, bridge appeared to hold.

book: 卷八, in a column-split case table** (row 1 `乙酉尚書`, row 2 `乙酉`, filed
under the heading 乙酉日甲申時), reading:
> 此日時遇年月純酉，從煞，己丑煞局，子辰化印，俱吉。

**從煞 — the same label 沈孝瞻 gives it, with the same 尚書 rank.** The first
verifier's negative control searched for a contiguous 8-character string and
was structurally blind to a column-split table entry — a documented, general
defect in 三命通會 extraction (see `BRIDGE_SEARCH_REGISTER.md` §6). **The second
finding stands; the first control has a mechanical hole.**

A third locus compounds this: 三命通會 卷三 reads the same chart under
納音/真長生/貴 — a third, unrelated frame:
> 如一命乙酉乙酉乙酉甲申，四水皆生於申，三貴又聚於申，自得長生，又在旬内，故大貴。

**Consequence:** 萬民英 himself holds **three mutually incompatible readings**
of this one chart across his own book — 納音貴 (卷三) / 從煞 (卷八) / 胞胎格
(卷十一). 沈孝瞻 (Qing) takes one of 萬's own two doctrinal sides (從煞, matching
卷八, not 卷十一). What looked like a clean cross-lineage disagreement is
**substantially an intra-source inconsistency, with a later author picking one
side of an internal split.**

A fourth locus, independently graded `INDEPENDENT_HIGH`, adds a genuinely
compatible fifth reading — 神峰通考's unsigned commentary layer reads it as
從煞 too (「乙木生于八月，酉金七殺…從煞為貴也」, matching 卷三's 納音 framing
on vocabulary grounds as `DIFFERENT_PROPOSITION_COMPATIBLE`, not a conflict),
and self-dates to ≥1589 — after 三命通會 (1578), so borrowing runs 神峰←三命,
not the reverse.

**BRIDGE_CLASSIFICATION**: `TRANSMISSION_DEPENDENT` — **recommended handling:
downgrade to Grade B pending resolution, not A.** It remains the single most
doctrinally productive chart in the corpus, but as a record of an *unresolved
internal tradition* rather than of two lineages disagreeing.

**PRODUCT_RELEVANCE**: this chart is a cautionary tale before it is evidence.
Any pipeline that consults 三命通會 by keyword or contiguous-string search alone
will silently miss the 卷八 従煞 entry and report a false negative exactly the
way the first verifier did. **A column-aware extractor is a prerequisite**
before any negative claim about 三命通會 coverage can be trusted (`BLOCKER
carried to §2 of the search register`).

**V2_IMPLICATION**: this chart is not yet usable to adjudicate the
CF-011 root-vs-following question, because its own primary source is internally

## What the matrix shows in aggregate

**Two axes are supported by every source that addresses them at all, with no
contradiction**: `RELATION_ACTIVATION` and `ROOT_EXISTENCE` (as a fact, not as
what it licenses). These are the closest things to `CROSS_LINEAGE_INVARIANT_
CANDIDATE`s the corpus has produced.

**`GLOBAL_STRENGTH` is the most contested row in the table** — supported as a
real chapter-length concern in two sources (任鐵樵, 陳素庵), used differently
(demoted to a precondition or a gate) in four (徐樂吾, 沈孝瞻, 淵海子平, 三命通會),
and **absent entirely** in one (窮通寶鑑). No other axis shows this range.

**`FOLLOWING_STRUCTURE` is supported everywhere but agrees nowhere** — every
source that addresses it has an opinion, and the opinions are the substance of
`CF-011`'s five positions. Breadth of attention is not the same as convergence.

**`格局 sequencing` is the row that most directly blocks S2** — every source
that states an order states a *different* order (or, in 三命通會's case, four
different orders within itself). See `SEQUENCING_COMPARISON.md`.

**`OUTLET_PRESENCE` and `TASK_CAPACITY`, the two axes S1.5 flagged as the
strongest single-lineage findings, remain single-lineage.** S1.6's bridge hunt
did not extend either — this is an honest gap, not a negative result, and is
the top item for a future S1.7-class effort focused specifically on these two
rows rather than on bridge-hunting generally.

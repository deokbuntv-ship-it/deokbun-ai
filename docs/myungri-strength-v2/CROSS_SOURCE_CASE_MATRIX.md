```
CROSS_LINEAGE_SAME_CHART_COUNT (vs 滴天髓闡微) = 0
```

62 non-滴天髓 charts were fingerprinted against all 129 charts in the S1 corpus.
**Not one match.** The lineages do not share worked examples with 滴天髓.

This is the most consequential limitation of S1.5 and it should be read before
anything below: **direct chart-level adjudication between 滴天髓 and any other
lineage is not currently possible.** Where the lineages disagree (`CF-011`,
`CF-012`, `CF-013`), they disagree *as doctrines*, and no chart yet exists in the
corpus that both have ruled on. That is a real evidential ceiling, not a
formatting problem.

Fingerprint method (§14): the four pillars concatenated, presentation direction
and spacing normalised away. Charts are identified by `CHART_CASE_ID`;
interpretations by source. Same fingerprint ⇒ same chart, **never** an automatic
merge of interpretations.

## CS-001 — The one genuine cross-lineage shared chart

**Chart:** 壬申 壬子 戊午 乙卯
**Lineages:** 子平真詮 (沈孝瞻, Qing) **and** 三命通會 (萬民英, Ming 1578)

| | 子平真詮, 三十三 論財 | 三命通會, 卷五 |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | 葛參政命 | not named |
| Category assigned | 財格 — 財露生官 | 財官格 |
| What the analysis is *about* | the **財 being exposed** (財露) | the **陽刃 under the Day Master** (自坐陽刃) |
| Diagnostic sentence | 如葛參政命，壬申、壬子、戊午、乙卯，**豈非財露？唯其生官，所以不忌也。** | 自坐陽刃，二壬申子財旺且多，子午雖衝，申子㑹午不能衝，時官星制伏陽刃，**只作財官格㸔**，所以大貴 |
| Resolving mechanism | 財 generates 官, so exposure is harmless | 官星 subdues the 陽刃 |
| Verdict | 貴 (a 參政) | 大貴 |
| Day-Master strength invoked? | **no** | **no** |

**What agrees:** the outcome (both 貴), and the category family (財/財官).

**What differs:** *what the chart's problem is taken to be.* 沈 sees an exposure
problem in the 財 and solves it by the 財→官 flow. 萬 sees a 陽刃 problem under the
Day Master and solves it by 官 controlling the 刃. Neither computes strength.

**Why this single case matters more than its size suggests.** It is the only
place in the corpus where two lineages address the same eight characters. Both
reach the same verdict by different routes, and **neither route passes through a
Day-Master strength assessment.** Two independent traditions, one chart, and the
strength question is not asked by either.

Two clash facts are also handled differently — 萬 explicitly reasons about 子午沖
being neutralised by 申子會 (申子㑹午不能衝); 沈 does not mention the clash at all.
Relation-level attention differs even where the verdict does not.

`VERIFICATION_STATUS`: **agent-retrieved from a collated edition (東里書齋) and
from the 四庫全書本 respectively; NOT independently re-verified by the main
session.** `donglishuzhai.net` refused connections when I attempted the check
(rate limiting; the host allows roughly one request per 30–60 s and had already
served four). This case is therefore `GRADE_B` pending re-verification and must
not carry a load-bearing conclusion alone.

**Also flagged:** the 三命通會 side comes from 卷五, not 卷八–九. That matters —
卷八–九's official rosters are partly later commercial-print interpolation, which
the 四庫 editors themselves flagged in 1779, and one entry names the 天啟 era
(1621–27), decades after 萬民英's death. 卷五 material is not subject to that
objection.

## The editorial-layer axis — a second matrix that turned out to matter more

| Layer | Date | 扶抑 | 調候 | 得時/失時 grid | 專旺 as 格 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 滴天髓 任鐵樵 | c.1848 | 0 | — | — | absent |
| 子平真詮 **沈孝瞻** | Qing | **0** ✓verified | **0** ✓verified | **absent** ✓verified | **0** ✓verified |
| 淵海子平 | — | — | — | — | absent |
| 三命通會 | 1578 | — | — | — | adjectival only |
| 窮通寶鑑 **base** | Qing | **0** | **0** | — | — |
| **徐樂吾** | **1936** | **present** | **13** | **present** | **present** |

Read down the last row. Every construct that modern practice treats as
foundational strength doctrine — the 扶抑 baseline, the named 調候 method, the
得時/失時/黨眾/助寡 grid, 專旺 as a pattern category — is **absent from every
classical source measured and present in the 1936 commentary layer**.

That is not an argument that 徐樂吾 is wrong. It is an argument that **he is a
named, dateable authority and must be cited as one.** `OBS-18`, `OBS-19`,
`OBS-21`.

**Two edition traps that make this easy to miss:**
- 8bei8's 子平真詮評註 has **inverted layer tags** in at least two chapters, and
  its "unannotated original" page is contaminated with 徐樂吾 material.
- Wikisource's 淵海子平 is an **abridged** edition missing 外十八格 and 神趣八法
  entirely — testing special-pattern doctrine against it returns a false negative
  for the whole layer.

Both were caught only because the researchers cross-checked against collated
editions. Neither is signposted on the sites themselves.

## Evidence for the DIRECT cells

**MONEY / CAREER ← AX-05 CAPACITY.** The corpus's money and office language is
overwhelmingly capacity-shaped and load-named, not strength-shaped:
- 足以用財 (`DTS-SHANGGUAN-06`, `DTS-GUANSHA-19`) · 日元足以任其財官 (`DTS-FANJU-13`)
- 財多身弱，所謂幫之則有功也 (`DTS-XINGXIANG-19`) — and its twin `-20` needs the
  *opposite* remedy
- 沈孝瞻: 有財用食生者，**身强而不露官** … 透官身弱，則格壞矣 — the wealth structure's
  viability, gated on a strength *precondition* inside the 格局 definition

**MONEY / CAREER ← AX-04 OUTLET.** `AC-10`'s two near-identical charts diverge on
outlet presence, and their *luck preferences invert* with it (前則從強，南北皆利；
此則木火，西北有害). Outlet presence changes which decades are favourable — which is
as downstream as it gets.

**TIMING ← AX-02, AX-03, AX-05, AX-06.** Every one of the 131 滴天髓 records spends
most of its length on 大運 behaviour (至X運…, 一交X…, 交X而亡). `OBS-10`. And AX-05
is explicitly time-scoped: 甲子癸亥，印旺逢生，日元足以任其財官.

**YONGSHIN ← AX-07 CLIMATE.** 窮通寶鑑 selects the favourable element entirely by
season and temperature and never by strength (`OBS-17`).

**YONGSHIN ← AX-08 格局.** 沈孝瞻: 八字用神，專求月令 — the 用神 is *defined* by the
月令 structure.

## Three findings that matter more than the grid

### 1. GLOBAL STRENGTH is INDIRECT to everything

Not one cell. That is the most consequential row in this document.

The corpus does contain a link from strength to outcome — but it is always
mediated. `DTS-XINGXIANG-19` and `-20` are **both weak** and need **opposite**
remedies (幫之則有功 vs 幫之反害), so knowing "weak" tells you nothing actionable
until you also know the structure. The 形象 chapter says so as doctrine:
均是衰也，幫之則凶，而助之則吉；或幫之則吉，而助之則凶.

**A global strength band is a poor predictor of every downstream question the
product actually asks.** If V2 ships one, it should not be load-bearing for advice.

### 2. RELATIONSHIP and BUSINESS are near-empty columns

Honest reporting: the corpus barely addresses them.

- **RELATIONSHIP** — 三命通會 卷七 論女命 is the only sustained material, and it
  reads 官 = 夫 / 食 = 子 through 十神 positions, not through strength. Its summary
  rule 日主旺相奪夫權而孤苦 is a strength claim, but a single one, in one lineage,
  in a frame whose assumptions are not ours. 滴天髓 mentions 刑妻克子 as *outcome
  narrative*, never as derived judgment.
- **BUSINESS** — `UNKNOWN` across the board. No classical source in this corpus
  addresses it as a category. Any V2 business judgment will be an extrapolation,
  and should be labelled as one.

**This is a scoping finding the product should hear:** two of six downstream
questions have almost no classical grounding in the material examined. That is
not a reason to refuse them — it is a reason not to claim classical authority
for them.

### 3. The most downstream-relevant axes are not the strength axes

Ranked by DIRECT-cell count: **AX-06 (4), AX-05 (3), AX-08 (3), AX-04 (2)** — and
AX-08 is 格局, which S1.5 concluded belongs *outside* the strength engine.

Global strength scores **0**.

## Consequence for §59

A standalone Strength engine would own the axes with the **weakest** downstream
links. The axes with the strongest links (special structure, task capacity, 格局,
outlet) either sit outside a strength engine or need a load ontology that does not
yet exist.

This is evidence for `PROVISIONAL_ENGINE_OBJECT = STRENGTH_AS_SUBSYSTEM_OF_MYUNGRI_STRUCTURAL_ENGINE`
— recorded in `S2_PRE_FREEZE_PACKAGE.md`, and a recommendation, not a decision.

## What this matrix cannot do

It reports **what the sources connect**, not what predicts outcomes. No claim here
is validated against real lives. Marking a cell `DIRECT` means a classical source
draws the link — nothing more.

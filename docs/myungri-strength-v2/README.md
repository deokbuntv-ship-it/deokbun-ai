## Previous gate: **S1.6 — CROSS-LINEAGE BRIDGE CASES** — `PARTIAL_BRIDGE_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`

S1.6 hunted for same-chart cross-lineage adjudication (S1.5's headline gap) across 8 search angles,
77 candidates, 28 adversarially verified. **4 Grade-A + 13 Grade-B + 1 Grade-C bridges survived** —
the raw target was exceeded, but `S2_PRE_FREEZE_READY` is still **NO**: the two conflicts the bridges
were meant to resolve (`CF-011`, `CF-012`) were **sharpened, not resolved** — 5-way and 3-way
respectively. Full accounting: [`S1_6_GATE_REPORT.md`](S1_6_GATE_REPORT.md).

The single richest expected vein — 任鐵樵 vs 徐樂吾 — turned out to be **largely a mirage**: 徐's own
front matter states his 滴天髓徵義 is a republication of 任's commentary under his own press, and his
genuine second work (滴天髓補註) deliberately does not re-analyse 任's charts. The real bridges came
from elsewhere — two new source families found this gate, 神峰通考 (張楠, Ming) and 千里命稿/呱呱集
(韋千里, 1935), which together contributed 5 of the 4 Grade-A bridges.

Three findings worth knowing before using anything here:

- **A correction to this program's own earlier claim.** An S1.6 draft stated 滴天髓 has no comparable
  sequencing order. It does — 任鐵樵's 八格 commentary gives 先觀月令 → 次看天干透出 → 再究司令以定真假 →
  然後取用. `CF-012` is now a confirmed three-way conflict between named authorities, not two.
- **CF-011 (root vs following) is now five-way**, not two — 淵海子平 (binary), 張楠 (yin/yang-asymmetric,
  the richest single passage found), 陳素庵 (root as one conjunct of a compound condition), 任鐵樵 (strength
  is the wrong axis), 沈孝瞻/徐樂吾 (傷食/印 are the real disqualifiers, not root).
- **Real cross-lineage doctrinal conflicts do survive adversarial testing**, not only compatible
  re-readings — 徐樂吾 vs 韋千里 dispute whether a 專旺 admission test sees hidden stems; 張楠 vs 萬民英
  assign the same water element opposite polarity *and* opposite quantity in the same chart. Two Grade-A
  bridges are genuine `SAME_PROPOSITION_DIFFERENT_ANSWER` conflicts, kept alongside the confirming
  evidence rather than downplayed.

⚠️ **Tooling warning, worse than S1.5 found:** the workflow caught a **fabricated quote submitted as
evidence** plus six further summarizer fabrications, and one Grade-A bridge dissolved into an
**intra-source inconsistency** once a table-extraction defect was found by a second independent
verifier. Every load-bearing quote in `CROSS_LINEAGE_BRIDGE_CASES.md` was obtained by raw retrieval;
`insource:` literal search on MediaWiki-hosted texts is the most trustworthy tool found. See
[`BRIDGE_SEARCH_REGISTER.md`](BRIDGE_SEARCH_REGISTER.md) §10–13 for the full environment-trap catalogue.

## Previous gate: **S1.5 — SOURCE DIVERSIFICATION** — `PARTIAL_SOURCE_DIVERSITY_SHORTFALL`

S1.5 added four lineages (子平真詮, 淵海子平, 三命通會, 窮通寶鑑) plus the 徐樂吾 commentary layer.
**146 records · 15 non-滴天髓 · 6 source families.** Full accounting: [`S1_5_GATE_REPORT.md`](S1_5_GATE_REPORT.md).

Three results that **correct S1 conclusions**:

- **R11 is `SCHOOL_DEPENDENT`, not "REFUTED".** 淵海子平 states three times that a root voids a
  following pattern; 滴天髓 has six charts doing the opposite (`CF-011`). Every S1 "REFUTED" was
  one-lineage and did not say so — a scope warning now heads the counterexample register.
- **CF-009 is resolved as `TASK_RELATIVE_COMPATIBLE`,** not a contradiction. A chart has no single
  classification *vocabulary*; its structural situation is stable across vocabularies.
- **The "classical" strength doctrine is substantially 徐樂吾, 1936.** 扶抑 as baseline, 調候 as its
  supplement, the 得時/失時/黨眾/助寡 grid, and 專旺 as a pattern name are all **absent from every
  classical source measured** and present in the commentary layer (`OBS-18`, `OBS-19`, `OBS-21`).

`S2_PRE_FREEZE_READY = NO` — the blocker is that **zero charts are adjudicated by two lineages**, so
no cross-lineage disagreement can be settled on a chart.

⚠️ **Tooling warning for anyone repeating this work:** all four researchers independently found that
WebFetch's summarizing layer **fabricates matches** — returning plausible "containing sentences" that
do not contain the searched term. Every count that matters here was derived by deterministic string
matching over raw DOM text.

## Previous gate: **S1 — SOURCE + CASE CORPUS BUILD** — collection **COMPLETE**

Build a professionally credible corpus of **real, source-provenanced worked natal cases** that a later
gate can use to discover structural axes. S1 collects evidence. **S1 does not decide doctrine.**

**Result: 131 records · 129 unique charts · 19 chapters · 0 fabricated · 21.4% independently spot-checked
with 0 discrepancies.** Zero doctrine declared, zero runtime code, zero rules adopted — as required.

Two things a reader should know before using anything here:

- **`SOURCE_MONOCULTURE_RISK = HIGH`.** All 131 records come from one lineage (滴天髓闡微, 任鐵樵), one
  edition. What looks like universal doctrine may be one author's habit.
- **`READY_FOR_S2 = NO`.** `CF-009` (a chart's classification depends on which question the chapter is
  asking) should be resolved first, because it bears on whether a single "strength axis" is the right
  thing to look for at all.

Full accounting, including six named weaknesses: [`S1_GATE_REPORT.md`](S1_GATE_REPORT.md).

## The three rules that make this corpus worth having

1. **Never fabricate.** No invented chart, no invented quotation, no reconstructed missing pillar. A
   truthful 72-case corpus beats a fake 100-case one, and the gate report states the real number.
2. **Source judgment and our normalization are separate fields, always.** If 任鐵樵 writes 「財多身弱」,
   that string goes in the source field verbatim. Our reading goes in a different field. Merging them is
   how a corpus quietly becomes a record of our own opinions — and how V1's circularity started.
3. **Collect, don't conclude.** S1 produces no rules. A pattern seen in three cases is not a rule; it is
   three cases.

## What is deliberately NOT here

No strength classifier · no seven-band mapping · no special-pattern verdict · no Yongshin · no runtime
code of any kind. Those belong to gates S2–S6, and only if the evidence supports them.

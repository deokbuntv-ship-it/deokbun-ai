## 1. PROPOSED_ENGINE_OBJECT

```
PROVISIONAL_ENGINE_OBJECT = STRENGTH_AS_SUBSYSTEM_OF_MYUNGRI_STRUCTURAL_ENGINE
                            (with a strong TASK_CAPACITY component)
```

**Why not a standalone Strength engine.** Four of five lineages do not treat
Day-Master strength as a first-class question (`S2_AXIS_CANDIDATES.md` §0), one
operates a complete doctrine with no strength axis at all (`OBS-17`), the
unifying strength-first framing is dated to 徐樂吾 1936 (`OBS-18`, `OBS-19`), and
global strength scores **zero DIRECT cells** in the downstream matrix.

**Why not pure task-capacity judges either.** `CAPACITY(party, load, ground, time)`
is the best-evidenced shape in the corpus (18/18 occurrences) but has **no load
ontology**, and its token evidence is single-lineage — 子平真詮 uses 便能受/當
instead of 足以. The *shape* recurs; the vocabulary does not.

**The honest position:** the evidence supports a structural engine in which
strength is one derived view among several, and in which the load-scoped capacity
relation is the most promising output shape. It does **not** yet support building
either one.

## 5. QUESTION_DOMAIN_MODEL

Not a chart property — **orchestration metadata**. CF-009's resolution
(`TASK_RELATIVE_COMPATIBLE`) shows one chart legitimately carries several
compatible descriptions, each bound to the question asked. Proposed output shape:

```
(question, chart) → conclusion + treatment_vector + evidence + uncertainty
```

Not `chart → band`. Emitting both 「金旺極，宜洩以水」 and 「從革獨象，喜化神水」 for
one chart is two correct answers to two questions, not a contradiction.

## 12. Recommendation

**Do not open S2 yet.** Two of the three S1.5 blockers are unresolved (CF-011,
CF-012) and both determine what S2 would be looking for. Resolving them needs
source acquisition, not analysis.

**If S2 opens anyway**, the safe subset is AX-02, AX-03, AX-09 and AX-10 — a
seasonal role assignment, relation presence, transparent evidence fields, and an
honest decline. That is not a strength engine. That it is the only safe subset is
itself the finding.

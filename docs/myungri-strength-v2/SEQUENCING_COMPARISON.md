## A correction to this document's own prior draft

An earlier version of this file stated: *"滴天髓闡微 (任鐵樵) — no stated
order... No comparable ordering statement exists."* **A systematic literal
sweep across the tradition's sequencing statements found this to be false**, and
the correction matters enough to record prominently rather than silently fix.

任鐵樵's 八格 commentary gives an explicit four-step pipeline:
**先觀月令 → 次看天干透出 → 再究司令以定真假 → 然後取用**, and his 衰旺
commentary explicitly forbids treating 得令=旺 mechanically, calling it a 死法.
This aligns 滴天髓 with the 月令-first camp (§2 below), not with "no doctrine."

This correction is itself evidence for a broader finding: **the tradition
prints at least five incompatible step-1 rules, and one single chapter of one
single book (三命通會 卷十) prints four of them without reconciling them.**
Sequencing should be surfaced to users as a live, contested design question —
not presented as settled classical doctrine in any direction.

## 1. What each lineage actually says — five positions, verbatim-verified

### Position 1 — 月令/格局 FIRST

**繼善篇** (as printed in 三命通會 卷十一; the 東海徐子平 attribution sometimes
attached to it is **not on the page**):
> 欲知貴賤，先觀月令乃提綱；次斷吉凶，專用日干為主本
> 取用憑於生月，當推究其淺深；發覺在於日時，要消詳於強弱

*(variant in 神峰通考: 先觀月令**及**提綱, 及 not 乃 — confirming this is a
widely reprinted verse, not one author's fixed wording.)*

**沈孝瞻, 子平真詮 論用神** (base text; ✓ layer-verified against the collated
東里書齋 edition — see `S1_5_GATE_REPORT.md` §4):
> 八字用神，專求月令，以日干配月令地支，而生剋不同，格局分焉
> 此皆由不知月令而妄論之故也
> 終以月令為主，然後尋用

The 秘本 witness adds the sharper form: seeking 用神 anywhere but the month
command is **執假失真**. *(Caveat on record: 東里山人, the modern editor,
argues this is over-read — 「觀此節，可知沈氏所謂之用神，非即是月令也」 — and calls
the 建祿月劫 passage 「沈氏自相矛盾之一端」. "A says 用神 = 月令" is not settled
even inside A's own lineage's reception.)*

**徐樂吾, 子平真詮評註 論用神** — formalizes a fallback ladder the base text
only gestures at:
> 先求之於月令之神…如月令無可取用，乃於年日時之干支中求之

**三命通會 卷十, 「㸔命口訣」, opener #1 of four (see §5):**
> 大凡㸔命先㸔月支有無財官方㸔其他

**任鐵樵, 滴天髓闡微 八格** (✓ corrected finding, see above):
> 先觀月令 → 次看天干透出 → 再究司令以定真假 → 然後取用

### Position 2 — DAY MASTER / STRENGTH FIRST

**明通賦** (東海徐子平 撰 / 萬育吾 解 — this attribution genuinely **is** on the
page, unlike 繼善篇's):
> 凡看命以日干為主，綂三元而配合八字干支
> 論運者以月支為首，分四時而提起五行消長

**萬民英's 解 on it** — he supplies a *mechanism* for the month's role and then
reads the couplet's own ordering:
> 大運以月支起，故月為提綱，看月支節氣淺深，四時得何節…先言日干，次言月支，舉其
> 所要者以示人也

**陳素庵, 命理約言 卷一「看命總法二」** — ✓ MAIN-SESSION VERIFIED (see
`S1_6_GATE_REPORT.md` §4). **The strongest, most explicit statement found in
the entire program**, and 陳素庵 stakes his whole method on it:
> 推命先看日干，或得時，或失時，或得勢，或失勢。
> 如此一一研究的確，然後用之為官煞，為財印，為食傷，其是強是弱，當用當捨，自然
> 精當無差，洞徹不惑矣。**此看命第一要訣也。**

And crucially, ✓ MAIN-SESSION VERIFIED, the audit is **recursive over every
pillar**, not the day stem alone:
> 不特日干而已，凡柱中干支皆當如此研究

**陳素庵, 「看命總法一」:**
> 看命大法，不過生剋扶抑而已。
> 總之以日干與財官等較其強弱，強者抑之，弱者扶之，不能扶抑者，以運扶抑之，其必不
> 可扶者則棄之，必不可抑者則順之。

**陳素庵, 「看正官法」** — strength is prior even *inside* a named 格:
> 看官之法，先論日干強弱。日干強則當扶官，日干若[弱]，則當扶日，再看官星得時
> 得勢與否

### Position 3 — 七殺 FIRST (張楠, signed 楠曰, his own voice)

**神峰通考 卷一 偏官格:**
> 凡看命先看七殺，若有七殺，就要將此七殺處置了，方能用得別物。若不能制去其
> 七殺，則殺星能害我性命。譬如人雖有金銀田產，無性命，此實為閒物。原書云：
> 『有殺須論殺，無殺方論用。』蓋先人立有此言，特未分明顯說，故使學者心上模糊。

### Position 4 — 從化 FIRST (the exclusive fallback ladder)

**神峰通考, 十干從化定訣** (byte-verified, MD5-matched by the workflow):
> 凡看八字，先明從化為本。從化不成，方論財官。財官無取，方論格局。**非格非局，
> 無足論已。**若從若化成格成局，必為富貴之命。賦云：火虛有焰，木盛多仁，得化
> 得從，定顯聲名之士，**惟甲木則無從化之理。**

**神峰通考 卷三 從化格** — 張楠 attributes this to a 賦, not to himself:
> 古人論造，先論從化，從化不成，方論財官。財官無取，方論格局。

**淵海子平 神趣八法 總釋 眉批** — located at `8bei8.com/book/yuanhaiziping_172.html`
after the original claimed URL proved a permanent 404. **It is a quotation, not

> 看八字先明從化為本，化不成方論財官，財官無取方論格局。如人身行藏興立，亦可
> 自立，終身無敗。從化成局則富貴。

**三命通會 卷十, opener #3 of four (see §5):**
> 凡命先論化氣

⚠️ **This one produced a REFUTED bridge candidate.** Read in context it is a

## 4. CF-012 restated, and one candidate REFUTED

### CF-012 as a live, sharpened conflict

> **P(淵海子平):** Determine 從化 first; only if it fails, consider 財官; only
> if that fails, 格局.
> **P(子平真詮):** Determine the 用神 from the 月令 first; consider 外格 only
> if the month yields nothing.
> **P(任鐵樵):** 先觀月令 → 次看天干透出 → 再究司令以定真假 → 然後取用 — 月令
> first, but with an explicit 真假 (special-pattern authenticity) check as
> step 3, not step 1.

`SAME_PROPOSITION?` — **YES**, all three answer *"what is decided first?"*, and
they give **genuinely different** answers: 淵海子平 screens special structure
before anything else; 子平真詮 defers it to last resort; 任鐵樵 places its
authenticity check in the *middle* of a 月令-first pipeline — a third position
neither pole fully states.

`CF012_CLASSIFICATION` = **SAME_PROPOSITION_DIFFERENT_ANSWER — a confirmed,
three-way sequencing conflict between three named or lineage-identified
authorities.**

### The candidate that was REFUTED, and why the refutation itself is instructive

A fourth apparent data point — 三命通會 卷十's 「凡命先論化氣」 read as agreeing
with 淵海子平's 從化-first order — was **adversarially tested and refuted**. In
context it sits among **four** parallel 「凡…先…」 openers in the same section
(財官 / 干神尅制 / 化氣 / 五行體面局勢), the very first of which — 「大凡㸔命先㸔
月支有無財官方㸔其他」 — states the **opposite** thesis using the same 方
fallback particle. And read closely, the 化氣 clause is not an ordering rule at
all: it is a mechanism for classifying a chart as 本體 or 化 by whether the
subject was born in day or night (a 天元變化書 rule), which is a different
question from "what do I decide first."

**Why this matters beyond the one refutation**: it demonstrates that a single
classical compilation can hold multiple, uncoordinated "first principle"
statements in one section, and that surface-matching a phrase (先論化氣 ≈
從化為本) without reading the surrounding argument manufactures a false
agreement. Any future sequencing research must read the full paragraph, not the
opening clause.

## 5. 三命通會 卷十 alone prints four unreconciled step-1 rules

This is worth stating as its own finding, because it undercuts the very idea of
a single classical sequencing doctrine more directly than any cross-lineage
comparison:

| Opener | Text |
|---|---|
| #1 | 大凡㸔命先㸔月支有無財官方㸔其他 |
| #2 | 先看干神有無尅制 |
| #3 | 凡命先論化氣 |
| #4 | 先論五行體面局勢 |

Four different nominees for "what comes first," inside one chapter, by one
author, with **no cross-reference or reconciliation between them.** This is the
single strongest piece of evidence in the program that "the classical
sequencing rule" is not a coherent object even within one influential text —
let alone across the tradition.

## 6. CF-011 and sequencing are entangled, not merely correlated

S1.6's root-vs-following bridge search (`CROSS_LINEAGE_BRIDGE_CASES.md`
BRIDGE-B2/B3) supplies the first worked-case evidence for the hypothesis raised
in the prior draft: **that the root disagreement (CF-011) may be downstream of
the sequencing disagreement (CF-012).**

Under a special-screen-first pipeline (淵海子平's own order), the root test
fires **before** any whole-chart 氣勢 assessment — so a rooted chart never
reaches a following branch to begin with, and the question "does root break
following" never needs to be separately asked; it is pre-empted by the
admission gate. Under 任鐵樵's practice — 月令 first, 真假 check third — the 氣勢
is read as a whole *before* a root's significance is adjudicated, which is
exactly the space in which he can rule a root present-but-non-fatal.

This remains a **hypothesis strengthened, not proven**, by BRIDGE-B2/B3: those
bridges show *modern* authors disputing 任's root-tolerant reading, but do not
themselves adjudicate whether their objection stems from a different
sequencing commitment or from an independent doctrinal preference.

## 7. What this means for V2

1. **`SEQUENCING_MODEL_STATUS = CONTESTED_BETWEEN_NAMED_AUTHORITIES`**, and
   more sharply contested than the prior draft understood — five distinct
   step-1 claims are on record, one classical author holds four of them
   simultaneously without reconciling them, and the previously-claimed
   "滴天髓 has no order" gap has been closed (with a correction, not a
   confirmation).
2. **Pipeline B (月令/格局 first, strength subordinate) is now the
   best-attested**, with three lineages including a corrected reading of 任鐵樵
   himself. This reverses the prior draft's implicit lean toward describing
   Pipeline A as the weakly-attested outlier — Pipeline A has a real, forceful
   Qing adherent (陳素庵) and should not be dismissed as a purely modern
   artefact.
3. **Order remains a lineage choice, not a settled fact.** Any V2 pipeline must
   carry `RULE_LINEAGE` metadata, or it will silently assert one school —
   quite possibly the wrong one, given how contested the field actually is.
4. **CF-011 may still be partly explained by CF-012**, and now has its first
   worked-case support (BRIDGE-B2/B3) rather than resting on doctrine alone —
   but the entanglement is not yet proven, only strengthened.
